Our Origin Story

We Built This Because We Were Sick of Burning Ad Spend

The internet is full of traffic generation experts, but almost no one is talking about what happens after the click. We got tired of watching brilliant campaigns die on the landing page, so we built the ultimate conversion engine to fix it.

Meet The Founder

The Most Expensive Leak in the
Digital World

My name is Laz Ilechukwu, and I know exactly how frustrating it is to watch hard-earned traffic bounce. After years in the trenches as a digital marketing specialist and scaling multiple businesses online, I realized a terrifying truth about this industry:

Founders and marketers are obsessed with the top of the funnel. They will spend tens of thousands of dollars on Facebook ads, SEO, and content creation. But when those highly qualified prospects finally land on the website... the momentum completely stops.

The prospect gets distracted. They have a minor objection. They decide they will "buy it later."

I watched my own businesses, and my clients' businesses, bleed revenue simply because our websites were passive. Standard pop-up tools were clunky, they slowed down page load speeds, and they completely broke when deployed on modern Single Page Applications. We needed a frictionless way to strike while the buyer's temperature was at its absolute highest.

So, we stopped looking for a solution and built one.
