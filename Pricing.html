Frequently Asked Questions (Read This Before You Buy)

Will adding Converstar slow down my website? +

Absolutely not. Converstar was engineered to be completely frictionless. Our single line of code loads asynchronously, meaning your website's text, images, and core functions load first, while our triggers load silently in the background. Your page speed remains lightning fast.

Does Converstar work on Single Page Applications (SPAs) or custom frameworks? +

Yes. Unlike legacy pop-up tools that break on modern tech stacks, Converstar's infrastructure flawlessly tracks dynamic routes and behavioral paths on React, Vue, Angular, and all modern SPAs without requiring hard page reloads.

What exactly is the 'Reseller License' in the Ultimate Bundle? +

It is a license to print money. When you upgrade to the Ultimate Bundle, you don't just get to use Converstar—you get the rights to sell it. We provide the Done-For-You assets, the white-label fulfillment support, and the training blueprints. You sell the software to your own clients or audience, and you keep 100% of the profits.

What does the 'Done-For-You Expert Setup' entail? +

If you purchase the Ultimate Bundle, we don't just hand you the keys and walk away. Our conversion experts will actively help you build out your first custom campaigns. We give you replication guidance and expert handoff so your system is completely optimized from day one.

I have clients. Can I keep their data separate? +

Yes. Both the Agency and Ultimate Bundle plans come with the Agency Super-Admin Account. This allows you to create completely unlimited, entirely separate client portals. Your clients' data, campaigns, and ROI tracking never mix.

Am I locked into a long-term contract? +

No. We don't force you into long-term contracts. You are billed monthly and can cancel your subscription at any time directly from your dashboard. However, once you see the immediate spike in your conversion rates, you will never want to turn it off.
